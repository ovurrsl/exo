## One rule that applies to every one of them

Every body states what was verified on Linux and what could not be verified
without Apple Silicon. Do not remove those sentences to make a PR look stronger.
A maintainer who finds an untested claim stops trusting the rest, and the whole
point of these is to be cheap to review.
