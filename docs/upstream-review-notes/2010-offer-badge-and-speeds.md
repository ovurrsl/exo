```markdown
I built the same feature in a fork before finding this PR, and yours is the
better base — `connection-type.ts` already derives Thunderbolt generation from
the `linkSpeed` string, which is the part I got wrong at first. So rather than
open a competing PR, here is what I have that this does not, in case any of it
is useful.

**An on-edge badge, not only a tooltip.** This PR surfaces the connection type
in the hover tooltip. In a cluster of more than three or four nodes the
interesting question is usually "which link is the slow one", and that is
answered faster by a short label sitting on the edge itself — `TB5`, `ETH`,
`Wi-Fi` — than by hovering each edge in turn. Mine renders a small badge at the
edge midpoint, offset perpendicular to the edge and scaled to the label width so
it does not sit on the line. Thunderbolt edges are drawn in the exo yellow and
Wi-Fi edges at reduced opacity, so the topology reads at a glance without
interaction.

**Nominal NIC speed alongside the measured throughput.** Your tooltip shows
Upload, Download, RTT/2 and Jitter, which is what the link is *doing*. The
complementary number is what it *could* do: the negotiated link speed and the
speed the hardware supports. A node sitting at 1 Gb on a 10 Gb card looks fine
in a throughput column and is obviously wrong when the two numbers are side by
side. I have that plumbed through from the Python side as two optional fields on
`NetworkInterfaceInfo`, `active_speed_mbps` (via psutil, platform-independent)
and `supported_speed_mbps` (macOS `ifconfig -v`, and I should say plainly that
the macOS parser is written against the documented format, not verified against
a real capture).

Against this branch the delta is roughly 40 lines: two fields on
`ConnectionType`, the badge rendering in the topology component, and the two
extra tooltip rows. I would rebase onto this branch and keep your identifiers
(`ConnectionKind`, `ConnectionType.kind`, `thunderboltGenerationFromLinkSpeed`)
rather than introduce parallel ones.

Want me to open it against this branch? Happy to split it — badge only, speeds
only, or both — whichever is easier to review. And if this PR is not moving, say
so and I will stop here rather than add to the queue.
```
